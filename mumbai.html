Around the hotel

What’s around the corner.

South Mumbai keeps its landmarks close together, which is most of the reason to stay in it rather than commute into it.

  • South Mumbai waterfront

    Gateway of India

    The basalt arch on Apollo Bunder, with the Taj Mahal Palace beside it and the Elephanta boats leaving from the steps.

  • Marine Lines, on the promenade

    Marine Drive

    The seafront walk South Mumbai takes in the evening, called the Queen’s Necklace for the curve its streetlights make after dark.

  • Fort, just inland

    Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus

    The Victorian Gothic terminus, a UNESCO World Heritage Site that is still a working railway station every morning.

  • Colaba, south along the bay

    Colaba Causeway

    Antique stalls, secondhand bookshops and old cafes, best walked slowly once the afternoon heat has gone out of it.

  • Every corner, all day

    Vada pav

    The city’s own sandwich — a spiced potato vada pressed into a soft pav, eaten standing up at the stall that fried it.

Journey times across South Mumbai depend on the hour and the traffic, so the front desk will give you a real one rather than a number printed on a web page.
