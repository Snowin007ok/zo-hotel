What we hear, and what we do

Six worries, answered

We translated six cancellation patterns in the booking data into the concerns a guest might have, then designed a response to each one.

Concern

“I might lose my money if my plans slip.”

What we do

Every Flexible booking is free to cancel up to 24 hours before check-in. No cancellation fee and no deduction — the full amount goes back to the card or UPI ID you paid with.

99% of non-refundable bookings in the industry dataset behind this project were cancelled anyway. In this data, locking guests in was not associated with fewer cancellations.

Concern

“My dates are not final yet.”

What we do

Move your dates twice on a Flexible rate with no change fee. You keep your room type where it is available, and if the new dates cost more you see the difference before confirming.

14% of changed bookings were cancelled, against 41% of bookings nobody touched. See the exit prompt built on this.

Concern

“Nobody has confirmed what I asked for.”

What we do

Parking, early check-in, a cot, a Jain meal — add it to the booking and we confirm each one in writing within two hours. If we cannot honour it, you may cancel free on any rate.

48% of bookings with no request attached were cancelled, against 22% of bookings carrying one special request.

Concern

“I found the same room cheaper somewhere else.”

What we do

Send us a public link within 24 hours of booking. If it is the same room on the same dates, we match that price and take another 10% off the matched rate.

41% of third-party bookings were cancelled, against 17% booked direct.

Concern

“I have no idea when my money comes back.”

What we do

We release the refund on the same business day you cancel and email a reference number. UPI lands in 3–5 business days, cards in 5–7, and those dates sit on your confirmation.

28% cancellation rate on no-deposit bookings, against 99% on non-refundable ones. The pattern suggests clear money terms sit better with guests than locked-in money.

Concern

“I am holding rooms for a group that has not decided.”

What we do

Hold up to five rooms for 48 hours with no card details and no charge. Confirm the ones you need and let the rest go, at no cost.

61% of group bookings were cancelled, the highest rate of any segment in the data.

Why this is believable

A flexible policy is only worth something if it is honoured.

Credibility

What you see is what you book.

  • Room details and amenities are shown before payment.
  • Rate conditions appear beside the price.
  • Prototype reviews and recognition are clearly labelled as fictional.

Reliability

  • Requests answered in writing within two hours.
  • Refunds released on the same business day you cancel.
  • If we cannot honour a confirmed request, you cancel free on any rate.

Transparency

  • The total you see includes taxes before you pay, not after.
  • Your cancellation deadline is printed on the confirmation and on the manage page.
  • We publish the data behind these claims, including the parts that look bad for us.
