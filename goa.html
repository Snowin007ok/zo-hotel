Around the hotel

What’s along the coast.

North Goa runs as one long shoreline, so a day out here is mostly a matter of picking which beach to walk to next.

  • The beach the hotel sits on

    Baga Beach

    Sand, shacks and fishing boats drawn up above the tideline, with the Baga river mouth closing off the north end.

  • The next beach south

    Calangute Beach

    Wider and busier than Baga, and the one to walk at sunset when the tide is a long way out.

  • South, above Sinquerim

    Fort Aguada

    The Portuguese fort on the headland, built to watch the river mouth, with its lighthouse still standing over the bastion.

  • North, over the headland

    Anjuna Beach

    Black rock and small coves rather than open sand, and the long-running flea market back from the shore.

How long any of it takes depends on the season and the state of the road, so the front desk will tell you rather than a number printed on a web page.
